The problem · non-revenue water

The fraud audits can't see.

A clandestine bypass is an illegal branch installed downstream of the legal connection: the customer keeps registering consumption, the branch is buried or embedded, and its hydraulic signature is masked by regular demand. Andean utilities consistently report that conventional location technologies perform poorly against this kind of theft.

The question behind this project: can a pressure wave, read by a single sensor, give away a T-junction nobody can see?

The physics · tref = 2·Lb / a

Every T-junction answers back.
Even buried.

The principle fits in four steps — and in 8.86 milliseconds.

STEP 1 / TRIGGER

Sharp closure

A PLC-commanded solenoid valve closes in ≈ 15 ms — well below 2L/a — generating a controlled water hammer.

ΔP = ρ · a · ΔV
STEP 2 / PROPAGATION

The wave travels

The pressure front runs along the PVC SCH-40 pipe at the Korteweg–Joukowsky celerity.

a ≈ 677 m/s
STEP 3 / REFLECTION

The bypass betrays

The T-junction splits the hydraulic impedance into two parallel branches and reflects a negative rarefaction pulse.

r = (Zeq − Z)/(Zeq + Z) = −⅓
STEP 4 / CLASSIFICATION

ML decides

The echo arrives at t = 2Lb/a. 17 features (time, frequency, db4 wavelet) are extracted and an ensemble returns the verdict.

Physical simulator

Generates transients with the analytic p(t) model, visualizes Signal/PSD, 2D spectrogram and 3D surface, wavelet scalograms — and predicts instantly.

6-stage filtering

Neighbor anti-spike, Hampel, IQR envelope, duration filter, manual suppression and zero-phase Butterworth. All off by default: the operator decides.

Synthetic + real

Class-balanced datasets, grouped CSV ingestion, ×4–10 augmentation (noise, jitter, scaling) and a 75/25 split with Suggest hyperparameter buttons.

4 models + ensembles

Random Forest, SVM-RBF, XGBoost and LightGBM under stratified 5-fold cross-validation. Multi-select: two loaded models become a voting ensemble.

Deep analysis

Confusion matrix, feature importance, Leave-One-Out analysis, per-fold metrics, outlier detection and removal, automatic model ranking.

Export and deploy

A single .joblib with model + scaler + feature list + metrics. The portable_v1 converter ships it as pure NumPy for the field device.

Phase 2 · Deploy — field device

The lab, in a case. An Arduino Q of the Linux class runs the FastAPI backend, samples the line at 500 Hz and runs the same 17-feature extractor as the desktop: bit-exact compatibility between training and inference. The technician only needs a phone.

Linux at the edge

The Arduino Q runs a full Python stack on board: NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn and PyWavelets. On-site inference, no cloud and no laptop.

500 Hz · 30 s

The same pressure sensor as the lab, read through the on-board analog interface. The full window is stored and processed locally.

Bit-exact

The extract_features routine is identical to the desktop's. Zero drift between what the model learned and what it sees in the field.

Voting consensus

RF + SVM + XGB + LGBM with soft voting. If the models disagree, the result is “inconclusive” and a retest is suggested — fraud demands certainty.

WebUI from the phone

On selecting a .joblib, /api/inspect_model instantly shows which models it contains. Big percentage, collapsible per-model detail, JSON verdict.

Field hardware

Relay, portable solenoid valve, buck converters and a battery with BMS in a sealed enclosure. It taps the line through a T and fires its own water hammer.
